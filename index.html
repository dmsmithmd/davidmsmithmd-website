Areas of expertise

Spine, cranial, and the questions that follow.

Clinical depth across degenerative and traumatic conditions of the spine and brain, applied to the issues that most often arise in litigation.

Spine

  • Degenerative spine disease: disc herniation, stenosis, spondylolisthesis
  • Spinal trauma and spinal cord injury
  • Cervical, thoracic, and lumbar surgery: decompression, fusion, instrumentation
  • Post-surgical complications

Cranial

  • Traumatic brain injury
  • Hemorrhagic stroke
  • Subdural and epidural hematoma

Medicolegal topics

  • Standard of care in neurosurgical diagnosis and treatment
  • Causation: acute injury vs. pre-existing degenerative findings
  • Timeliness of diagnosis, referral, and surgical intervention
  • Future medical care needs and surgical recommendations
  • Informed consent and surgical decision making
